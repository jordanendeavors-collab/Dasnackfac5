Please read this part

This is not an allergy tool. If a child has a food allergy, the package label and the family’s own judgment are the only things to trust. This app cannot see cross-contact warnings such as “made in a facility that also processes peanuts,” and when a product’s ingredient list is missing from the database, a dangerous item can look clean. Never let it be the last check.

A parent made this, not the school. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed by the school, the district, any teacher, or any brand named in it.

It does not replace reading the label. Manufacturers reformulate without notice, and packaging in your cart is the only current source.

Where the product data comes from. Barcode lookups use Open Food Facts, a free crowd-sourced database made available under the Open Database License. Entries are contributed by the public and can be incomplete, out of date, or simply wrong.

The quality score is a rough guide, not advice. It is one parent’s scoring of ingredient lists and nutrition numbers — not nutritional, dietary, or medical advice. For anything that matters to a child’s health, ask a pediatrician or a registered dietitian.

Brand names are for identification only. All trademarks belong to their owners. Nothing here implies a relationship in either direction, and no brand paid for or reviewed its listing.
